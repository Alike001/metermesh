## Video direction

Use the Broadside frame system remixed to MeterMesh black, white, and verification green. Real product captures remain the visual truth inside sharp frames. Large lowercase statements and mono proof labels carry the silent story. Reveal each item on its reading beat and hold the last two seconds of every frame still. Avoid synthetic UI, invented metrics, gradients, soft shadows, rounded SaaS cards, crypto decoration, slideshow front-loading, screensaver drift, bouncy entrances, and any payment or Mainnet implication.
